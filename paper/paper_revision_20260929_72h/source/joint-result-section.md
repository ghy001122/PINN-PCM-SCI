The full-history gradient was admitted before training. A custom first-order VJP preserves the continuous dependence through the reversal states (T_r,T_pr), resistance and all future RC voltages. It traverses the history events in reverse without updating history during backpropagation. For v_(n+1)=a_n v_n+b, a_n=1-h(1/R_L+1/R_n)/C, the backward voltage recursion is λ_n=z_n+a_n λ_(n+1), and the RC path contributes λ_(n+1) h v_n/(C R_n²) to the resistance gradient; direct v²/R and v/R dependencies are retained separately. Selected discrete reversal indices, signs and clipping boundaries are fixed within this branch derivative. No claim of smoothness at event changes or second-order differentiability follows.
