2. **Information boundary.** Insert the finite observation-equivalent family and fixed-temperature thermal restriction. The new theory figure separates mathematical invariance, necessary repairability and a physical lower bound.

5. **Numerical and data limits.** This development has one base initialization, the original reference and one common native reader. Public complete-data access and synthetic-material limitations remain explicit.
