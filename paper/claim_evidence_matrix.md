## 2. Reviewer-risk self-audit

| Risk | Severity | Evidence-based control | Residual boundary that must remain explicit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A title or abstract implies a successful PINN study although no PINN was trained. | High | Use the terminal numerical-contract/benchmark-limits identity and state `NO_ORACLE_EVENT_OR_PINN_EVIDENCE`. | The work closes before method development; no accuracy, speedup, or generalization claim is available. |
| Q0 is mistaken for driven oracle qualification. | High | Report Q0 as a zero-drive guard and retain `PENDING_S2_CROSS_RUN_ADJUDICATION`. | Q0 has no event vote and cannot validate the driven electrothermal-defect chain. |
| The QN RuntimeError is interpreted as physical nonexistence or PDE invalidity. | High | Label it `VERIFIED execution failure` and name the frozen inner-Newton limit. | Solvability under another contract and physical behavior remain `UNKNOWN`. |
| Reduced-fixture diagnostics are promoted to scientific results. | High | Isolate them under `NON_SCIENTIFIC_DIAGNOSTIC` and outside the ledger. | Their mesh, duration, and role differ from production; only the failure mechanism is diagnosed. |
| The finite-difference check is overgeneralized. | Medium | Report its exact state-specific discrepancy and limitation. | It did not reveal a large mismatch in the tested direction; it does not exclude errors in untested directions or states. |
| COMSOL route language is read as a legal accusation. | High | Preserve “required PASS not established” and cite the exact bounded review. | No conclusion is made about the user's license, any other institution, or all jurisdictions. |
| CTH is marketed as globally novel. | High | Keep `positive architecture novelty not cleared`; list direct ingredient precedents. | Absence of an exact bundle collision in 13 carriers is not priority evidence. |
| Synthetic coefficients are mistaken for measured material constants. | High | Mark every `SYN_EDT_2D_V1` coefficient as `ENGINEERING`. | No material fit, experiment, or source-aligned parameter identity exists. |
| Dirty-tree revision is treated as a sufficient code snapshot. | High | Publish contract, manifest, and artifact hashes; report revision `f2c727...` plus `dirty=true`. | Exact uncommitted source identity is not recoverable from the revision alone; this limits independent bitwise code reconstruction. |
| Compute reporting appears complete despite missing RAM measurement. | Medium | Report wall time, process CPU, core-hours, hardware, and `peak_ram_bytes=null`. | No peak-RAM claim is allowed; VRAM is zero because execution was CPU-only. |
| Later unexecuted ladder rows are shown as failed numerical comparisons. | High | Use `NOT_REACHED`, not empty result cells or inferred failures. | Only intent 2 failed; intents 3–13 have no outcome other than non-execution. |
| Failure is silently repaired by replay or threshold tuning. | High | Preserve the atomic claim, consumed budget, `rescue_attempts=0`, and no-rerun rule. | Any changed solver study would be a new contract and cannot supersede this result retroactively. |
| Formal statistical language survives although formal was never opened. | High | Omit confidence intervals, effect sizes, power claims, and ranking tables. | The preregistered formal design is a plan only and supplies no evidence. |

## 3. Final self-check

- Every numerical value in the main tables maps to an existing report, manifest, or explicitly non-scientific diagnostic.
- No unexecuted case is represented by an invented or empty numerical result.
- The legal/source decision is bounded and non-accusatory.
- Synthetic physics is labeled engineering, not experimental.
- Q0, execution failure, diagnostic interpretation, and unknowns remain separate layers.
- No oracle, event, PINN, CTH increment, OOD, formal, or experimental claim survives the audit.
