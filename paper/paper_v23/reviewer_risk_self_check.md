# Reviewer-risk self-check

## Major risk 1: “There is no successful PINN result.”

Correct. P0 was conditional on T0 carrier competence and was not triggered.
The present package must be positioned as a failure-analysis and solver-recovery
study, not a positive PINN method paper. A journal expecting a new successful
algorithm would reject the current evidence.

## Major risk 2: “LF3 is just logit distillation plus class balancing.”

The components have prior art and are cited. The draft does not claim either
component as original. The only reported result belongs to the complete
combination. A future latent-mechanism claim requires the preregistered matched
output-phase ablation; historical LF2 is not a strict single-factor control.

## Major risk 3: “Why reject a model that passes the extra-fine event guard?”

The evaluator guard checks event existence, locality, peak, and recovery. The
full-medium carrier gate additionally requires at least 0.90 support recall.
LF3 passes the former and fails the latter. The distinction is substantive and
predeclared, not post hoc.

## Major risk 4: “Why is direct interpolation so much stronger?”

The full medium field is available, making direct interpolation a strong and
honest comparator. LF3 does not beat it. A future neural route must either meet
accuracy noninferiority or predeclare and demonstrate a different measurable
role such as sparse-data reconstruction, compression, inverse inference, or
complete-protocol OOD generalization. None is retroactively claimed.

## Major risk 5: “The recovery sequence was adapted after each failure.”

Yes. V2.2R through LF3 is a sequence of separately frozen, terminal campaigns,
not one factorial experiment. It supports failure localization and hypothesis
refinement, not unbiased ranking across every intervention. The manuscript says
so explicitly.

## Major risk 6: “Single seed and fixed discretization are insufficient.”

Agreed. The result is development evidence. Multi-seed, formal OOD/stress, and
continuum/material validation are excluded. The stress references remain sealed
and unread because no candidate reached them.

## Major risk 7: “The malformed launcher exit record undermines the run.”

The raw record is preserved rather than rewritten. Normal scientific completion
is supported by a terminal summary, fixed update count, final checkpoint,
prediction, audits, batch ledgers, and matching remote/local hashes. The defect
is limited to post-run wrapper logging and did not trigger a repeat trajectory.
