Status: `LF7_MATCHED_SCREEN_INCOMPLETE_IDENTITY_INVALID`

This package presents PHK-V2.2R--LF7 as a bounded failure-analysis and
competence-first solver-recovery study. The strongest positive mechanism result
remains LF4's matched teacher-interface exposure effect. LF6 then showed that a
98.7% reduction in the fixed blind physics objective can erase a safety-valid
localized event carrier.

LF7 asked whether a smaller fixed step or a medium-audited rollback filter could
preserve that carrier. The identity-valid P0-S control completed 1200 updates:
the blind objective fell from `4.927872` to `2.971883` (`ratio=0.603076`, above
the required `0.50`), while cycle 1 disappeared and cycle-2 recall/recovery fell
to `0.0762/0.0282`. P0-F rejected its first four 25-update proposals on field
preservation, accepted the fifth at `eta0/16`, and then encountered rollback
state-identity drift caused by Adam-state snapshot aliasing. It has no valid
endpoint, so LF7 cannot attribute a filter-mechanism effect.

The terminal outcome is `LF7_MATCHED_SCREEN_INCOMPLETE_IDENTITY_INVALID`,
candidate is none, and the unique next is
`RETAIN_VALID_ARM_NO_MECHANISM_ATTRIBUTION`. The P0-S result is a valid bounded
