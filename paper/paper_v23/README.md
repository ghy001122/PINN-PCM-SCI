competence-first solver-recovery study for a coupled electro-thermal phase-field
PINN benchmark. It is an advisor-reviewable paper draft, not a positive-method
submission.

The strongest positive mechanism evidence is the matched teacher-interface
exposure effect. LF10 reproduced its positive recall direction in all three
materialized sampling streams (17/23/29), with quality preservation in two and
median `delta_Rmin=0.04628`. The second headline result also replicated: pure
strong-physics continuation caused event degradation in all three streams,
while only two crossed the frozen residual-ratio gate and none produced a

for ordinary Adam and the medium-gradient projected arm. Neither reached the
required 200 accepted updates; projection supplied no extended feasible path.
Full refinement and its conditional control were therefore
`NOT_RUN_PREREQUISITE_NOT_MET`, not failed. Post-shutdown threshold analysis
left the formal outcomes unchanged, and direct `LF_ONLY` led the mean
symmetric-difference predicate in all 375 role-grid comparisons. These are not
