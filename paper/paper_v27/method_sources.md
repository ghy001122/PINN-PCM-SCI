## Bounded source check for the remaining electrical/readout interface

This was a targeted check during the fixed conditional runs, not a new field-wide review or an implemented second alternative. It changed no running objective, endpoint rule, data access or budget.

- [Patel et al., cvPINNs, original manuscript §3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.05343) formulates neural residuals through space-time control-volume flux balance, incorporating prescribed inflow fluxes and approximate quadrature. The [published article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2021.110754) is JCP 449, 110754 (2022). This establishes prior art for integral/discrete physics learning; its hyperbolic-system guarantees and entropy mechanisms do not transfer automatically to this electrothermal cell.
- [Doğan et al., CoFINN v1, §5 and Eqs. 15–20](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.06587v1) explicitly uses CFD-derived target fluxes as a supervised regularizer. That information role excludes direct adoption under this project's sparse-only physics boundary. An own-field electrical balance with known BC would require a separate comparison; its efficacy and novelty are unproved here.
- [Um et al., Solver-in-the-Loop, NeurIPS 2020 proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/43e4e6a6f341e00671e123714de019a8-Abstract.html) establishes prior work on learning through differentiable PDE solvers. Its learned correction of iterative solver trajectories differs from eliminating the present instantaneous elliptic voltage subproblem. The official abstract was read for that limited distinction; it does not validate a new PCM hybrid, its information fairness, or its gradient implementation.

Access: the cvPINN original PDF and relevant CoFINN HTML method sections were read. No linked implementation was installed or run. The cvPINN HTML URL was unavailable; its PDF was used. A separate 2024 unified-FV publisher page returned 403 and was not used for detailed claims. This is a limited prior-art screen, not an exhaustive priority search.
