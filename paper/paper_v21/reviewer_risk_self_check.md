# Reviewer-risk and claim-boundary self-check

## Executive verdict

The package is internally a complete bounded negative qualification study. It is not the originally intended positive PINN-method paper. The publishable story is reference/oracle qualification and failure preservation; it should not be marketed as a new PINN architecture result.

## R1. Is the main result merely an implementation failure?

**Answer:** No. Both implementation defects were reconciled without changing a scientific value, and all 14 scientific solver intents completed with zero execution or hard-guard failure. The terminal cause is a completed component-wise convergence comparison: event-time F→XF exceeds M→F.

**Evidence:** terminal summary, S1 closeout, two amendment records.

## R2. Does exact replay prove oracle validity?

**Answer:** No. Exact replay verifies deterministic reproducibility of one frozen calculation. It cannot establish refinement convergence across different grids and time steps.

## R3. Is the event-time failure numerically tiny enough to ignore?

**Answer:** The absolute times are close, but the frozen rule concerns contraction, not visual closeness. The finer difference is about 1.37 times the preceding difference. Ignoring it after seeing the result would be outcome-adaptive.

## R4. Could five passing components outweigh one failure?

**Answer:** No. All six were preregistered load-bearing endpoints. Aggregation would allow stable bulk fields to hide an unstable event endpoint that is central to switching claims.

## R5. Does no PINN experiment weaken the manuscript?

**Answer:** It limits the manuscript to benchmark qualification/numerical limits, but is scientifically required by the contract. Adding neural curves using an unqualified floor would be a stronger flaw.

## R6. Are Sharp-PINNs and PF-PINNs reproduced?

**Answer:** No. Their paper, repository, and clean-room identities were audited prospectively, but S2 was not opened. The manuscript only cites them as planned method anchors and related work.

## R7. Is adaptive pseudo-time a KC baseline result?

**Answer:** No. It was a planned generic residual-relaxation falsification control. It was not run, and it is mechanistically distinct from a field-selective physical-time warp.

## R8. Does the control suite prove physical causality?

**Answer:** Only in a bounded synthetic sense. Nominal event plus Joule-off no-event supports that Joule heating is necessary in this frozen object. Other controls show retention or geometry sensitivity under their exact interventions. None validates a real material.

## R9. Is the object a PCM material model?

**Answer:** No. It is a dimensionless, literature-inspired electrothermal phase-field wall-cell. No named-material calibration, parameter inference, experimental comparison, or commercial-device identity is claimed.

## R10. Is the candidate floor usable?

**Answer:** No. The carrier is retained to make the failed comparison auditable. Its own field floor_sealed_and_converged=false prohibits neural consumption.

## R11. Could the route be rescued by another threshold, grid, or interpolator?

**Answer:** Possibly, but this is UNKNOWN. Any change would define a new prospective numerical contract and cannot rewrite PHK-V2.1.

## R12. Does 14/14 completion mean qualification passed?

**Answer:** No. Execution completeness, guard validity, event validity, and oracle qualification are separate layers. The first three completed; the fourth failed.

## R13. Are the two implementation amendments post hoc scientific tuning?

**Answer:** No scientific quantity was changed. One corrected a tuple/list representation in a no-event Boolean using immutable source values; the other mapped labels position-for-position. Both are separately recorded and hashed.

## R14. Are compute comparisons made?

**Answer:** Only S1 CPU accounting is reported: 1.128515625 core-hours, no GPU, zero failed solver intents. No neural compute or cross-paper speedup is claimed.

## R15. Does the study prove a universal weakness of PINNs?

**Answer:** No. It proves no PINN success or failure. It demonstrates a workflow that stops before PINN when the reference process is not qualified.

## R16. Does the result meet the desired positive Q2-method-paper target?

**Answer:** No. It is a defensible negative benchmark-qualification manuscript, not evidence that two new PINN modules beat strong baselines. Journal quartile and acceptance remain outside the evidence.

## R17. Are unpublished stages described as results?

**Answer:** No. D/I/formal pools, Sharp/PF targets, strong raw, bottleneck arms, PHA, KC, GPU and formal OOD are labeled NOT_REACHED.

## R18. Are all material unknowns explicit?

**Answer:** Yes:

- whether a different prospective numerical contract restores event-time convergence;
- whether Sharp/PF metrics reproduce locally;
- whether a qualified strong raw is competent;
- whether PHA-MF, KC or their interaction improves any endpoint;
- whether findings translate to real PCM materials/devices.

## Mandatory wording

Use:

> All nominal trajectories were event-valid, but the event-time refinement component was not oracle-valid under the frozen contract.

Do not use:

- the oracle nearly passed;
- Sharp/PF were unnecessary or inferior;
- PINN training failed;
- PHA/KC did not work;
- the real device requires Joule heating in the same way;
- the method is experimentally validated;
- the result guarantees a Q2 publication.

## Final disposition

No P0 claim-boundary violation remains in the package. The remaining author-only work before submission is ordinary metadata: funding, competing interests, author contributions and acknowledgements. It does not alter any scientific result.
