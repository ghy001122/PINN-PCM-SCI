# PHK-V2.1 claim–evidence matrix

## Status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED:** directly supported by immutable local carriers.
- **SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION:** a bounded inference from verified comparisons.
- **UNKNOWN:** not answered by the executed contract.
- **NOT_REACHED:** downstream stage was never opened because an upstream gate failed.

## Implementation amendments

Two implementation defects were reconciled transparently:

1. Intent 2 no-event carrier: tuple/list representation mismatch; Boolean recomputed from the immutable original two-cycle data; no solver rerun.
2. Terminal labels: short labels mapped position-for-position to frozen V2.1 labels; no reorder, recomputation, or scientific-value change.

These records support traceability only. They do not upgrade any scientific claim.

## Claim ceiling

The strongest defensible conclusion is:

> PHK-V2.1 is a complete, bounded, synthetic oracle-qualification No-Go. Its nominal trajectories are event-valid, but the frozen event-time refinement component is not oracle-valid. No baseline, PINN, PHA-MF, KC, GPU, formal OOD, material, device, or experimental evidence was produced.
