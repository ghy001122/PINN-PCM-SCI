## Intro

We propose a hybrid pulse scheme for multilevel phase-change memory (PCM) combining partial RESET and staircase SET pulses to enable efficient and precise conductance tuning. By leveraging statistical convergence through fixed pulse sequences, this approach eliminates iterative feedback and complex control logic inherent in conventional write-and-verify (W&V) schemes. Demonstrated on a 40-nm PCM chip, the method achieves 32 reliably distinguishable conductance states with 4.11 pulses per state on average – establishing the fastest convergence at record precision (32 states) for single-cell multilevel PCM. This represents a 98% pulse reduction versus state-of-the-art methods at equivalent precision. This hardware-efficient scheme demonstrates strong inter-device consistency across unscreened devices, enabling scalable deployment for neuromorphic accelerators.
