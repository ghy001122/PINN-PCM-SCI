## Intro

In this paper, a mesh-free method based on physicsinformed neural network (PINN) is presented for time-domain electromagnetic simulations. This PINN method employs the weighted Laguerre orthogonal polynomials (WLPs) based Maxwell's equations as governing equations, which leads to unconditional stability. A numerical example of the rectangular waveguide verifies the effectiveness of the proposed method.
